# YouTube to MP4

> Fast, private, browser-based video converter for creator-owned, public domain, and Creative Commons videos.

## Overview
YouTube to MP4 (https://ytubeconvert.com) is an ultra-fast, privacy-first web utility built for video creators, educators, and archivists. It enables users to convert YouTube videos into MP4 video (360p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K) or extract audio as MP3 for offline viewing, academic research, and fair-use educational purposes.

## Core Features
- High-Definition Video: Convert YouTube to MP4 in 720p, 1080p, 1440p, and 4K Ultra HD.
- Built-in Video Player: Preview converted files directly in the browser before downloading.
- Zero Registration: No user account, email, or credentials required.
- High Performance: Sub-second page loads powered by Astro SSG on Cloudflare Pages.
- Privacy Architecture: No permanent file retention; conversion outputs are automatically expunged within 15 minutes.

## Acceptable Use Policy
- Users must only process content they own, content licensed under Creative Commons, public domain works, or material for which explicit permission has been granted.
- The service actively enforces limits: DRM-protected material, paid YouTube Movies/rentals, and private videos are strictly barred from processing.
- Respect copyright law and third-party terms of service at all times.
