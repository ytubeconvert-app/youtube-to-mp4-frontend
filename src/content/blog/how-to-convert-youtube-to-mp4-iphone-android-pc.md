## 1. How to Convert YouTube to MP4 on PC & Mac (Desktop)

Converting YouTube videos on desktop environments (Windows, macOS, and Linux) offers the simplest experience because desktop web browsers provide native support for modern file system dialogues and high-throughput streams.

### Method A: Using Our Cloud Web Converter (Zero Installation)

1. **Locate the Video**: Open YouTube in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or Brave). Navigate to the video you have permission to download.
2. **Copy the Clean URL**: Highlight the full address in your browser address bar (e.g., `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s8_1FhJ0x34`) and press `Ctrl + C` (or `Cmd + C` on Mac). Alternatively, click the **Share** button below the video and choose **Copy Link**.
3. **Open YouTube to MP4**: Navigate to our homepage or [YouTube to MP4 Converter](/youtube-to-mp4-converter).
4. **Paste the URL**: Click into the input field and press `Ctrl + V` (or click our **Paste** button).
5. **Select Your Quality Profile**:
   - For general presentations or mobile playback: **720p HD** (fastest download, modest file size).
   - For high-definition monitors and crisp text: **1080p Full HD** (recommended sweet spot).
   - For high-end video editing and 4K displays: **4K Ultra HD** (largest file size).
6. **Click Convert**: Our high-speed cloud pipeline extracts the clean audio and video streams and merges them into an industry-standard H.264 / AAC MP4 container.
7. **Preview and Save**: Test the video inside the built-in [YouTube to MP4 player](/youtube-to-mp4-player), then click **Download MP4** to save the file into your local `Downloads` folder.

## 2. How to Convert YouTube to MP4 on iPhone & iPad (iOS 15 to iOS 18+)

Apple’s iOS enforces a secure sandbox architecture, which in earlier years made downloading external files tricky. However, starting with iOS 13 and Safari's native Download Manager, saving MP4 files on your iPhone is effortless.

### Step-by-Step iOS Workflow:

1. **Copy the Video Link in the YouTube App**:
   - Open the YouTube app on your iPhone.
   - Tap the **Share** arrow beneath the video player.
   - Tap **Copy link**.
2. **Launch Safari**:
   - We strongly recommend using **Safari** on iOS rather than in-app browser viewports (like Instagram or Twitter browsers) because Safari possesses direct access to the Files app.
3. **Visit the Converter**:
   - Open [YouTube to MP4](/) in Safari.
   - Long-press in the input bar and select **Paste**.
4. **Select Resolution & Convert**:
   - Select **1080p** or **720p**.
   - Tap the **Convert** button.
5. **Confirm Download in Safari**:
   - When the file finishes processing, Safari will display an iOS modal dialog: *"Do you want to download 'video_1080p.mp4'?"*
   - Tap **Download**.
6. **Accessing Your Video on iPhone**:
   - Look at the bottom-left or top-right of your Safari address bar. You will see a small circular blue download arrow indicator.
   - Tap this arrow to see your download progress.
   - Once completed, tap the file to open it, or tap the magnifying glass icon to reveal it directly inside the iOS **Files** app (`Files > Downloads`).
   - *Pro-Tip*: To move the MP4 to your iPhone **Photos** camera roll, open the file in the Files app, tap the **Share** icon in the bottom corner, and tap **Save Video**.

## 3. How to Convert YouTube to MP4 on Android Devices

Android offers a straightforward file system that makes video conversion fast and accessible.

### Step-by-Step Android Workflow:

1. **Copy the Share URL**: In the YouTube app, tap **Share** > **Copy link**.
2. **Open Chrome or Firefox**: Launch Google Chrome on your Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, or Android tablet.
3. **Paste and Choose Format**: Open [YouTube to MP4 Downloader](/youtube-to-mp4-downloader). Paste your copied URL and tap **Convert**.
4. **Download the MP4 File**: Once processing completes, tap **Download MP4**. Chrome will show a bottom snackbar showing *Downloading file...*
5. **Locate the Saved File**:
   - The file automatically appears in your notification shade.
   - You can also view it anytime via the **Files by Google** app or your phone’s **Gallery** under the `Download` album.

## Troubleshooting Common Download Problems

| Symptom | Probable Cause | Verified Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Download stuck at 99%** | Network packet loss or aggressive browser cache | Clear browser cache, reload page, and re-trigger conversion. |
| **Video plays but no sound** | Video source uses separate audio DASH stream | Use our converter which automatically merges video (H.264) and audio (AAC) tracks using FFmpeg. |
| **URL not recognized** | URL contains tracking parameters or playlist IDs | Strip everything after `&` in the link. Use clean URLs like `youtube.com/watch?v=...` |
| **Error 429: Too Many Requests** | Exceeded IP rate limits | Wait 60 seconds before initiating another batch conversion. |
